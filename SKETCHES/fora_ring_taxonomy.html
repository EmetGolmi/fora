FORA · Civic Ring Taxonomy

Two independent systems coexist on every user: the ring progression (behavior-based, earned through platform contribution) and the gold ring (credential-based, awarded on external license confirmation). The military ribbon is separate from both and recognizes service independently.

Gold ring sits outside the progression entirely. Only licensed professionals with a state-confirmed credential receive a gold ring. It is not earned by activity — it is awarded on verification. A licensed PE who never posts still has a gold ring. Gold does not convert to silver or platinum. The credential pill (PE, MD, ESQ, etc.) is shown on the gold ring only.

How sector rings are awarded: FORA tracks contribution quality within each CivicIssue domain. When a user crosses the quality threshold in a single sector (measured by upvote weight, amendment participation, steelman quality, and peer recognition), they earn that sector’s colored ring. When they cross the threshold in a second sector, the colored ring converts to silver. Platinum is awarded algorithmically when contribution quality is exceptional across the full platform — rare, and never self-reported. Sector ring history is preserved on profile. In-context display shows one ring only — the current highest tier.
Military ribbon · independent of the ring system · coexists with any ring tier · DD-214 verified

The ribbon is not a ring. Military service is recognized through a branch insignia ribbon that sits below the avatar. It carries only branch colors — no text on the marker itself. The colors are the signal. Verified via DD-214 upload. Completely independent of the ring progression — a veteran earns their branch ribbon regardless of whether they have a sector ring, silver, platinum, or gold ring. All combinations are valid.
