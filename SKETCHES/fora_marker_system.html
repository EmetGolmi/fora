Layer 1
Sponsor ring
Dashed arc in sponsor's brand color. Absent if no sponsor. Labeled below with "Sponsored · [Org]".
Layer 2
Credential ring
Gold double-ring = state-verified license (PE, ESQ, MD…). Silver = self-reported. None = anonymous.
Layer 3
Service badges
Small icon badges at top-right. Judge ⚖️, civic body 🔨, first responders. Stack left for multiples.
Layer 4
Avatar face
Occupation abbreviation, emoji, or AI-generated patriot caricature. User-chosen. Always a mask, never a photo.
Layer 5
Ribbon bar
Veterans only. Crimson/gold horizontal band beneath the marker. DD-214 verified. No ribbon for non-veterans.
The credential badge (ESQ, PE, eng, 🍺) is pinned to the bottom of the circle and is always visible regardless of which layers are present. It is the one non-negotiable readable element at every size.

Ring weight = epistemic weight
The ring communicates how the credential was established — not whether the person is trustworthy, but what kind of claim is being made. Uppercase abbreviation = state-verified. Lowercase = self-reported. Emoji = expressive category.

04 · Contribution Score
Independent of credential. Always visible.
Quality · not volume
The score runs 0–1000. It is calculated from quality signals — source tier, steel-man completion, position change over time, discourse contributions rated useful by peers. Volume cannot game it. A non-licensed engineer with a score of 820 outranks a PE with a score of 140 in every sponsorship tier and in the civic record's sorting logic.

05 · Sponsorship Ring
Ambient. Disclosed. Never vocal.
Sadie Hawkins · user approaches sponsor
The sponsor ring is a dashed arc in the sponsor's brand color, outermost layer, labeled below with "Sponsored · [Org]" in 6px JetBrains Mono. The sponsored user speaks. The sponsor is present. Like a jersey sponsor — visible but not playing.

Default state
Civic institutions (bar associations, unions, veterans organizations, professional associations) pay no platform fee — their offering is aligned with the mission. Commercial sponsors pay a platform fee that is disclosed in the picker. The user sees what their marker will look like before accepting. They can switch or drop a sponsor any month with no penalty to their score.
