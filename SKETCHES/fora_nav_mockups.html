How should FORA navigate?

Three distinct navigation models. Same content, different architecture. Pick one — then we build the full dashboard.

Option A
Top Navigation
Sections live as tabs across the top nav bar. Maximum horizontal real estate for content. Clean, minimal. The nav disappears when you're inside a section — all focus goes to the content.

Familiar pattern — every user already knows how top tabs work. Zero learning curve.

−
No persistent personal anchor — your identity, followed jurisdictions, and active items disappear when you navigate to a section.
−
Scales poorly — adding a sixth or seventh section makes the tab bar crowded.
Option B
Left Sidebar
Navigation lives in a persistent left sidebar. Your civic identity anchors the top of the sidebar — always visible, always yours. The sidebar can collapse to icon-only on demand. Deep hierarchy lives here: followed jurisdictions, tracked bills, active deliberations.

Option C
Both — Top nav + Left sidebar
Top nav carries the five major sections. Left sidebar carries personal items — your identity, followed jurisdictions, tracked bills, active deliberations. Sidebar is narrower (160px) since it holds only personal content, not full section navigation. Sidebar collapses on content pages to free up width.
