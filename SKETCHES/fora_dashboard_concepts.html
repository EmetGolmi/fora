★ This day in Philadelphia · May 11
1880 — The Free Library of Philadelphia is founded
Dr. William Pepper convinces City Councils to charter a free public library — one of the first in the United States. The Parkway Central building wouldn't open until 1927, but Pepper's argument anchored the idea: knowledge belongs to the commons.
"A library is the people's university." — William Pepper, 1880
Famous Philadelphians · Born today
Marian Anderson · 1897
Contralto, civil rights pioneer, born in South Philadelphia. In 1939, after the Daughters of the American Revolution refused her Constitution Hall, she sang from the steps of the Lincoln Memorial to 75,000 people — and a radio audience of millions. First Black soloist at the Met.
