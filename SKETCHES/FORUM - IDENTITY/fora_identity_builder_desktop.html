C
Residential address
Why we ask for your full address
Your zip code helps us show you the platform, but it isn't precise enough to determine your local representatives. Zip codes were created for mail delivery, not political boundaries. A single zip code can cross multiple cities, counties, congressional districts, and school districts. Your representatives at every level — federal, state, county, city, school board — are determined by your exact residential address. FORA uses the same address standards as voter registration.
Help America Vote Act · 52 U.S.C. § 20901 · National Voter Registration Act · 52 U.S.C. § 20501
Street address is optional. Publicly shown as region only (e.g. "Mid-Atlantic" or "Alexandria VA").

Why occupation shapes your civic identity
Your occupation is your public civic identity. A Master Electrician commenting on building code carries a different perspective than an anonymous post — not more validity, but different context. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez was a bartender before Congress. A bartender commenting on alcohol licensing has direct standing. Everyone who lives somewhere has a stake in how it is governed.

Why licensed professionals are a distinct civic category
All individuals licensed by a state to perform their jobs share a common foundation: they are legally authorized by a government agency to work in their occupation, having met predetermined standards of competency, training, and education. These licenses are time-limited credentials designed to protect public health, safety, and welfare by ensuring a minimum standard of professional practice.
