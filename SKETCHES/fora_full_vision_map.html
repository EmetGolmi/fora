The business model
Tom Sawyer's fence
The civic layer is free. That's the fence. Professionals pay to operate where their clients already are. Advertising that feels like participation, not intrusion. Institutional contracts for organizations that need their members informed.
The moat
Trust · built first
Nobody built the connective layer because trust in civic infrastructure is the hardest problem in technology. FORA started there — with government data, the most verifiable, nonpartisan foundation that exists. Everything else unlocks from that.
Ten years
Essential like utilities
Every jurisdiction in the world runs the same broken infrastructure. The platform that standardizes civic and professional life becomes essential the way utilities do — you don't think about it. You simply cannot operate without it.
◆ The mission · in Noah's words
"I want to help people look in the right places — guide them toward organization, proficiency, self-determination, education. And I want to reconnect people. Create a real community again. It was still somewhat safe to just acknowledge a stranger passing you on the sidewalk. Now everyone is terrified to talk to each other. Being informed is critical to our nation's survival and our way of life. We force people to acknowledge the other's opinion — it's the decent thing to do. You can always agree to disagree. But you cannot govern yourself in ignorance. FORA is going to be huge."
