⚖ CARE Principles — Indigenous Data Sovereignty (applied to §9 Language & Recording)
C
Collective Benefit
Data ecosystems must serve the collective needs of Indigenous communities. Tribal language data benefits the community, not only researchers or the platform.

Authority to Control

v12 Changes — Non-Governmental Powers, Psychological Analysis, Scientific Method Layer, Sentiment Gradient
NEW §12 Non-Governmental Power & Psychological Layer — 10 new entities across two new sections. §12a: the entity model for the 75+ non-governmental powers that affect extraordinary numbers of people outside formal governmental structures. §12b: the psychological analysis and collective reasoning layer — AI-generated, community-refined, scientifically structured.
NEW NonGovernmentalEntity: the 75+ powers. Distinct from CommunityOrganization (citizen-led, neighborhood scale) and CivicBody (formal government body). Covers: militant/paramilitary organizations, unrecognized polities, religious institutions, transnational corporations, financial institutions, intelligence agencies, international bodies, lobby organizations, NGOs. entity_class enum distinguishes type. power_basis enum describes where authority comes from (military · financial · religious · legal · informational · social). is_recognized_state for entities like Kosovo, Taiwan, Somaliland that govern territory without full international recognition. headquarters_jurisdiction_id nullable — some entities (ISIS remnant, Al-Qaeda) have no fixed jurisdiction.
NEW NGEDesignation: who has designated what as what, under what authority. designating_entity_id (the country or body making the designation) → target_nge_id. designation_type: fto (Foreign Terrorist Organization) · sdgt (Specially Designated Global Terrorist) · sanctioned · proscribed · monitored. Partial designations handled: the EU designated only Hezbollah's military wing until 2013 — scope_note captures this. Multiple countries can have multiple designations of the same entity with different scopes. All are in the record.
NEW NGERelationship: how non-governmental entities relate to each other and to governments. relationship_type: sponsored_by · funds · controls · is_wing_of · headquartered_in · allied_with · opposes · governs_territory · infiltrates · competes_with. claimed_by enum — some relationships are claimed by one party and disputed by the other. The Hezbollah/IRGC relationship is claimed differently by Iran, Hezbollah, the US, and independent analysts. All claims are in the record.
NEW PsychologicalAnalysis: the AI-generated + community-refined psychological analysis attached to CivicEvents and CivicIssues. Structured by the scientific method: observation_text · question_text · research_summary · hypothesis_text · evidence_summary · analysis_text · conclusion_text · communication_notes. Each field corresponds to a stage of the scientific method. Version-controlled (version integer). Permanently attributed — every version has a generated_by (AI) or approved_by (professional) field. community_refined boolean tracks whether human contributors have materially changed the AI draft. The AI regenerates a new version incorporating confirmed community additions — but its regeneration is itself a versioned record that can be challenged.
NEW PsychologicalContribution: citizen and professional additions to the psychological analysis. credential_type: licensed_psychologist · psychiatrist · social_worker · researcher · citizen. Credential is verified via CredentialVerification (§4). professional_contributions are visually distinguished in the UI. contribution_type: addition · correction · challenge · context · counter_hypothesis. The community can challenge any element of the AI analysis — the challenge is visible alongside the challenged text, permanently. Wikipedia problem avoided: attribution is permanent, consensus cannot be captured anonymously, professional credentials are verified and displayed.
NEW RadicalizationPathway: a documented psychological pathway linking research literature to specific event patterns. pathway_name: cumulative_radicalization · lone_actor · cell_based · state_sponsored · grief_driven · ideological_conversion. stages jsonb: array of {stage_name, description, warning_signs, intervention_points}. linked_literature: array of {author, title, publication, year, doi}. The pathway connects CivicEvent → CivicIssue → CivicDefinition → PsychologicalAnalysis. Ghazali maps to grief_driven + cumulative_radicalization. The pathway shows where the process could have been interrupted. It is not an excuse — it is a mechanism that, if understood, can be interrupted upstream.
NEW ScientificMethodMapping: maps each contribution and content element on a CivicEvent or CivicIssue page to one of the seven stages of the scientific method. stage enum: observation · question · research · hypothesis · experiment · analysis · communication. anchor_type/anchor_id points to the content being mapped (Contribution, Source, PsychologicalAnalysis, etc.). In "reasoning view" the page reorganizes by stage rather than by layer (government/media/citizen). Citizens can see the full reasoning structure of how the community is thinking about an event — what's observation, what's hypothesis, what's been challenged.
NEW SentimentGradient: replaces like/dislike counts. A warmth/coolness score derived from engagement quality — not raw counts. warmth_score (0.0–1.0) derived from: useful votes, steel-man completions, successful cluster additions, professional engagement. cool_score (0.0–1.0) derived from: upheld challenges, tone intercept triggers, relevance flags. Neither score is displayed as a number. Both scores drive a CSS visual treatment: warm contributions develop a barely perceptible amber edge glow; challenged contributions develop a faint cool blue. The gradient only develops through genuine engagement over time. Cannot be gamed by bots the same way counts can — it's a felt quality, not a scoreboard. NEVER displayed while the citizen is actively reading — only visible on the contribution card in the list view.
SEM The Scientific Method as platform architecture: every CivicEvent page has seven structural layers that map to the seven stages. Observation = government layer (what happened). Question = the CivicIssue it connects to. Research = CivicDefinitions, historical precedents, RadicalizationPathway literature. Hypothesis = citizen Perspective and Concern contributions. Evidence = citizen Evidence contributions and multi-source examinations. Analysis = ClaimClusters, PsychologicalAnalysis, challenged hypotheses. Communication = the permanent record, shared globally. This is not a metaphor. It is the page structure in reasoning view.
NEW User: Memorial fields — deceased boolean · deceased_at · memorial_name · memorial_phrase · memorial_flame · memorial_tribute_enabled · designated_contact_user_id. When deceased = true: the semi-anonymity of life is lifted, contributions carry the full name, a gilt flame SVG renders beside the name on every contribution in perpetuity. memorial_phrase defaults to "May their memory be a blessing" — user can set in advance in any language (zichronam livracha · رحمه الله · Requiescat in pace). The flame cannot be removed, cannot be sponsored, cannot be customized. It belongs to the memory. Memorial tributes are Perspective-only, uncontestable, steel-man gate suspended — this is not discourse, this is witness.
SEM The would-be perpetrator use case: the radicalization pathway is documented. Interruption points are in the early stages — grief, isolation, grievance. FORA is structurally anti-isolation. A citizen whose grief is witnessed, whose anger has a legitimate outlet in the civic record, who can contribute a Connection contribution and have 23 people engage seriously with it — may diverge from the pathway. The platform cannot stop a determined operative. It can change the conditions that produce them.
v11 Changes — Civic Knowledge Layer: Issues, Events, Contribution Upgrade
NEW §11 Civic Knowledge Layer — 9 new entities + 1 breaking upgrade. The platform now has three levels of civic knowledge: Action (formal legislative/executive object) · CivicEvent (real-world occurrence, citizen's entry point) · CivicIssue (persistent public question). Citizens enter through events, are walked toward formal Actions, and contribute at any level.
NEW CivicIssue: persistent public question across time and administrations. "Presidential war-making authority" emerged in 1787 and is still active. parent_issue_id enables hierarchy from specific to general. plain_language_summary for citizens with no prior knowledge. status: active · dormant · resolved · contested.
NEW CivicEvent: real-world occurrence as citizens experience it. "US strikes Iran." "Deportation of Venezuelan nationals." status: developing · ongoing · resolved · contested · classified. legal_authority_established boolean drives UI prompt when no formal Action has been connected. is_war_powers_relevant boolean flags the 60-day clock.
NEW IssueAction / IssueEvent / EventAction: junction tables with typed relationship fields. Not just "these are connected" but how: authorized_by · challenged_by · addresses · limits · expands · instance_of · historical_precedent. EventAction.claimed_by captures who asserts the legal connection — executive, legislative, judicial, journalist, citizen. Multiple parties can claim different connections to the same event.
CRIT Contribution — BREAKING UPGRADE: anchor_type enum (action · civic_event · civic_issue) replaces action_id FK. Contribution is no longer exclusively anchored to legislation. contribution_type enum: evidence · perspective · concern · connection. is_multi_source boolean + examination_question for Multi-Source Examination mode. claim_text for AI extraction. cluster_id for conscience-gathering. tone_intercepted records whether the citizen saw the counter-argument before posting.
NEW ContributionSource: sources attached to a contribution — one for single-source, many for multi-source examination. excerpt (3-sentence max) displayed prominently, not buried. excerpt_context is the reading-between-the-lines annotation. methodology_note is where the Gaza number conversation becomes productive: Hamas · Lancet · IDF methodology side by side. photo_order enforces sequence as argument. position controls examination display order.
NEW ClaimCluster: the conscience-gathering unit. The AI continuously clusters contributions by structural claim similarity across the entire platform. representative_claim is a neutral synthesis of what all contributors in the cluster are arguing. opposing_cluster_id surfaces the strongest counter-cluster. cohort_breakdown shows who holds this position — anonymous, aggregate, for self-awareness not manipulation.
NEW RelevanceChallenge: steel-man-gated challenge mechanism. steel_man_first is REQUIRED before challenging — you must write the strongest version of what you're challenging. challenge_type: relevance · scope · methodology · factual · connection_disputed. Status: open → upheld/dismissed/withdrawn. All outcomes are public record.
NEW CivicDefinition: the modal glossary. Every term in civic discourse that citizens might not know — AUMF, cloture, RCO, excess mortality — is tappable. plain_language_definition + historical_example grounds abstractions in events. related_action_ids links from the definition to actual legislation. jurisdiction_scope adapts the definition to context.
SEM The four contribution types map to distinct intellectual acts: evidence = what is, with a source. perspective = what I think it means. concern = what I fear it leads to (explicitly labeled uncertain). connection = the thread between this and something else. These are displayed, clustered, and challenged differently. They are not "comments" — they are structured contributions to a civic record.
HYG The learning funnel: citizen reads "US bombs Iran" → opens FORA → finds CivicEvent → plain-language description + War Powers alert → clicks into CivicIssue "Presidential war-making authority" → sees history back to 1787 → clicks into 2001 AUMF as Action → sees contributions at all three levels → contributes → gets clustered with others making the same argument → sees the strongest counter-cluster. That is civic education. Not a civics class. An experience.
v10 Changes — Community Organizations & Efforts
NEW §10 Community Organizations & Efforts — 10 new entities: CommunityOrganization, OrgMembership, OrgMeeting, MeetingInvite, Effort, EffortParticipant, EffortGovernmentTarget, EffortEvent, EffortDocument, EffortFollow, SisterOrgLink.
NEW CommunityOrganization: citizen-led org distinct from CivicBody (which is government/quasi-government). org_type enum covers neighborhood associations, unions, watershed councils, faith communities, etc. issue_tags drives global discovery. meeting_schedule jsonb drives scheduling UI. verified boolean drives badge and elevated submission trust.
NEW OrgMembership: role-based permissions within an org. chair = can schedule meetings and submit Efforts to government. secretary = can approve minutes. Representative = can speak on behalf of org in discourse. committee field supports sub-committee structure (e.g. BVNA Transportation Committee). public_role boolean — members control their own visibility.
NEW OrgMeeting: meeting scheduling with agenda (jsonb array linking to Effort records), hybrid location support, invite_method enum (fora / email / sms / all). CivicRecording pipeline wired in — auto-transcription, action item extraction, AI-generated minutes reviewed and approved by secretary. Approved minutes are immutable.
NEW MeetingInvite: granular invite tracking per recipient. Non-FORA members invited by phone/email receive one-click join link. user_id retroactively set if invitee creates FORA account. contact_value stored hashed, never exposed in UI.
NEW Effort: the organizing unit of civic action. status enum: forming · active · submitted · stalled · resolved · withdrawn. stalled is not failure — it's honest. resolution field (free text) is the most important field on a resolved effort: the org describes what actually happened. public boolean: false while forming, true once submitted to government — all submitted efforts are permanent public civic record.
NEW EffortGovernmentTarget: tracks submission to specific agencies/officials independently. target_type supports civic_body, office, person, and agency_external (free text for agencies not yet in FORA's government stack). submission_status per target: not_submitted → submitted → acknowledged → responded → rejected → referred.
NEW EffortEvent: the civic record spine. event_type: milestone · meeting · design_work · document · submission · endorsement · response · site_visit · stalled · resolved. actor_labels jsonb supports both FORA user/org references and free-text labels for non-FORA participants and historical reconstruction.
NEW EffortDocument: document vault with version control. doc_type: sketch · drawing · map · application · letter · petition · photo · reference · minutes · resolution · other. is_official boolean makes documents immutable once formally adopted. document_date separate from created_at for historical uploads. supersedes_id for version chains.
NEW SisterOrgLink: the global discovery layer. link_type: sister_city (activates dormant municipal relationships) · shared_issue (system-suggested from tag overlap, user-confirmed) · formal_partnership · informal_connection. shared_tags captures the specific issues connecting two orgs. Status lifecycle: suggested → pending_confirmation → active → dissolved. Enforced uniqueness: org_a_id always < org_b_id.
CRIT CommunityOrganization: +local_label string — jurisdiction-aware display term. FORA is the adapter, the conduit through which people relate and learn from one another. The schema is universal; the language is local. RCO in Philadelphia. Community Board in New York. ANC in DC. Civic Association in Virginia. FORA speaks the civic language the citizen already knows, everywhere it operates.
HYG Effort vs. Action distinction: Action (§5) is a legislative or governmental act — a bill, a vote, a resolution. Effort (§10) is a citizen-led undertaking directed at a government target. An Effort may reference one or more Actions (e.g. a Slow Zone application that eventually produces a formal city Action), but they are different objects with different lifecycles and different ownership.
