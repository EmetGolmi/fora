What BVNA reviews
Zoning Variance
Deviation from code that would cause "unnecessary hardship" if enforced strictly. Use variances (wrong activity for zone) or dimensional (height, setback, lot coverage).
Decided by ZBA · 1515 Arch St, 18th Fl
Special Exception
Permitted by code but requires applicant to prove no negative neighborhood impact. Examples: take-out restaurants, short-term rentals, group homes.
Decided by ZBA · BVNA vote carries weight
Civic Design Review
Large projects reviewed for how they relate to the public realm. No zoning change required — triggered by building size or location. CDR committee makes recommendations to L&I.
Reviewed by PCPC CDR Committee · Monthly
Liquor License & Sidewalk Cafés
New liquor licenses referred by PLCB. New sidewalk cafés introduced as City Council ordinances. BVNA holds meetings on both — neighbors can formally object or support.

Explained
Quick answers
What is a zoning refusal? ▼
When L&I reviews a permit application and the proposed project doesn't conform to the zoning code, they issue a refusal detailing the violations. The applicant can then withdraw, revise, or appeal to the ZBA.
Does BVNA's vote decide the outcome? ▼
No. BVNA's advisory letter is a recommendation, not a decision. The ZBA makes the final call. But BVNA's position carries significant weight — and neighbors can attend the ZBA hearing directly to be heard as interested parties.
What's "as-of-right" development? ▼
Projects that fully conform to the zoning code get permits automatically — no RCO meeting required. If you see new construction BVNA hasn't held a meeting on, it's likely as-of-right.
How does BVNA's vote work? ▼
Show of hands — near neighbors (within ~200 ft, who received mailed notice) vote first, then the community at large. If a clear consensus in favor can't be reached, BVNA opposes. The tally and summary go into the advisory letter.
What are provisos? ▼
Conditions negotiated between the applicant and the community at the RCO meeting. Must be specific enough for L&I to enforce. Private agreements (not in the ZBA record) are still binding if signed — but unenforceable by the City unless formalized through an official mechanism.
During construction
Street use permits

These are out of scope at the zoning meeting — but they are legally required by the City. If a permit hasn't been pulled, report it to 311.

🚛 Dumpster on street or sidewalk ▼
Two permits may apply: a Temporary Construction Dumpster Permit from the Streets Dept (call 215-686-5500) and an L&I Construction Dumpster License via eCLIPSE. Each dumpster must display an RFID medallion. No servicing 9 PM–7 AM. phila.gov ↗
🏗 Scaffolding, boom, lift, fence on ROW ▼
Requires a Street Closure Permit (Equipment Placement type) from Streets Dept, applied online at least 10 business days in advance. If a travel lane is blocked, police presence is required. phila.gov ↗
🏗 Crane on public right-of-way ▼
Street Closure Permit plus a separate Crane Placement Application with a signed Police Approval Application. Tower cranes also need an L&I Tower Crane Permit via eCLIPSE. $15M liability insurance required. phila.gov ↗
📬 7+ day full closure — neighbor notice ▼
Contractor must notify all properties within 540 ft at least one week prior — by mail, email, or door-to-door. Must include duration, hazards, parking restrictions, detours, and contact info. (Philadelphia Code § 11-705(c.1))
