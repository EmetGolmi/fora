◆ Understanding this stat
What does attendance mean?
Every senator is expected to show up and vote. 1,247 of 1,364 means Fetterman voted on 1,247 out of 1,364 total votes held while he was in office.

Missing votes isn't always avoidable — illness, family emergencies, travel conflicts happen. But a pattern of missing votes can signal disengagement from the job.

Fetterman missed more votes during his 2023 hospitalization for depression, which he disclosed publicly.
PA Senate average attendance: 92% · Fetterman: 91%
◆ Understanding this stat
What does party-line voting mean?
Party-line voting measures how often a senator votes the same way as the majority of their party.

68% means Fetterman voted with most Democrats 68% of the time — and independently 32% of the time. For context, the average Senate Democrat votes with their party 84% of the time.

Fetterman is notably independent for a Democrat — he has broken with his party on immigration, Israel policy, and several trade votes.
Senate Dem average: 84% · Fetterman: 68% — among the most independent Democrats
◆ Understanding this stat
What are committee assignments?
Before any bill reaches a full Senate vote, it goes through a committee — a small group of senators who hold hearings, call witnesses, amend the bill, and decide whether it moves forward.

A committee chair can kill a bill simply by never scheduling a vote. This is where most of the real legislative work — and legislative blocking — happens.

Fetterman's Agriculture committee seat means he has a direct say over food, nutrition, farm, and rural policy. His Banking seat gives him influence over financial regulation.

What is "cost of getting the message out"?
Running for Senate costs money — staff salaries, advertising, travel, printing, campaign events, and digital outreach. This is the total amount raised and spent by Fetterman's official campaign committee for his 2022 Senate race.

$4.2M is actually below average for a Pennsylvania Senate race ($6.1M average). Most of his funding came from small individual donors and progressive organizations.

This is not his personal income or salary. Senate salary is $174,000/year.
Source: FEC.gov filings · 2022 cycle · Official campaign committee only
