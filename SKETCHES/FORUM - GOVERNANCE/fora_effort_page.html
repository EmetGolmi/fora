May 2018
Milestone
Transportation Committee formed — Noah Goldman joins
May 23
Eugene Desyatnik welcomes Noah Goldman (RA, AIA) to the BVNA Transportation Committee. Outgoing chair Joe Celentano transitions. Active issues identified: curb cuts at Rite Aid, 7th/Passyunk sight lines, 11th St bike lane, stop signs at Montrose/Nebinger.

June 2018
Design work
First aerial sketches — 7th & Passyunk pedestrian refuge
Jun 8
Noah Goldman produces aerial overlays of 7th/Passyunk identifying triangular pedestrian refuge zone, proposed crosswalk locations, fire hydrant constraints, and sight line issues. References Janette Sadek-Khan / NYCDOT sneckdown approach and NACTO speed table standards.
Noah Goldman · RA Eugene Desyatnik

Committee meeting
Site visit — 7th, Passyunk & Carpenter
Jun 21
Committee meets on location. Joe Alkus raises concern: proposed triangle doesn't resolve mid-block crossing between Nebinger and Santore Library. Nate Hammitt notes manhole cover constraints. Matt Skolnik requests additional signage at library crossing.
Noah Goldman Nate Hammitt · AIA Joseph Alkus · BVNA Safety Matt Skolnik Eugene Desyatnik
Design revision
Iterated sketch — crosshatch crosswalk, pedestrian/parklet delineation, MMAX colorway
Jun 22–29
Multiple design iterations respond to Alkus concerns. Stop signs retained. Pedestrian egress zone separated from restaurant parklet by color. MMAX Corundum pavement product researched. Trigonometry calculated for lane widths. Request sent to Traffic Engineering for survey CAD files.

July 2018
Agency meeting
On-site with Traffic Engineering — 7th & Carpenter
Jul 31
Eugene Desyatnik and Joe Alkus meet with Philly Traffic Engineering at the intersection. City reviews tools available: plaza app, speed humps, signals, pedestrian refuge, added crosswalks. Outcome: crosswalks at 7th/Passyunk will require removing up to 3 parking spots per crossing (6 total). City to follow up.
Eugene Desyatnik · BVNA Joseph Alkus · BVNA Traffic Engineering · Philly Streets
Design — final iteration
sk3 — triangular crosswalk with egress/parklet delineation, all stop signs retained
Jul 8–9
Final sketch iteration produced. Stop signs remain at all existing locations. Crosshatch crosswalk pattern proposed — unusual for Philly. Inspiration: Oakland Chinatown, Zurich, NACTO design guides. Indego bike share siting explored for refuge zone — NACTO siting guide reviewed for clearance requirements.
Noah Goldman Nate Hammitt

December 2018
Pivot
Effort shifts to Slow Zone Program — two applications required
Dec 12
Matthew Solomon volunteers to file Slow Zone application for Bella Vista North. OTIS advises Christian St cannot be included (two-way with center line) — two separate applications required for North and South zones. Only 2 Slow Zone awards planned citywide for 2019. SOSNA, PSCA/EPX also competing.
Matthew Solomon · Applicant Eugene Desyatnik · BVNA
Submission
Slow Zone application filed — Bella Vista / Queen Village North
Dec 13
Matthew Solomon submits completed application to Charlotte Castle, Vision Zero / OTIS. Application includes: contact form, Slow Zone boundary map (South–11th–Christian–4th), Traffic Safety Concern Form, letter to Councilman Squilla requesting signature, ~20 signatures gathered. BVNA board asked for letter of support.
Matthew Solomon · Lead applicant Eugene Desyatnik · BVNA

Endorsement
Ward committee support — all South Philly applications endorsed
Dec 13
Mark Kasten reports from ward committee meeting: ward will support all slow zone applicants from the ward (not just one). Ward includes neighborhoods throughout South Philly east of Broad. Mark Kasten to request formal ward letter of support for BVNA application.
Mark Kasten · Ward Committee
February 2021
Design work
601 Christian Pedestrian Plaza — fresh drawings, speed tables for level crosswalks
Feb 8
Effort revived at 6th & Passyunk under new scope. Noah Goldman produces architectural drawings for a pedestrian plaza at the triangular parcel. Speed tables designed for level crossings. Dan Rosin (601 Christian property owner) confirms he and partner Rafi Licht are supportive and had previously been asked by Streets to extend curbs — cost was the barrier.
Noah Goldman · RA Eugene Desyatnik · BVNA Dan Rosin · Property owner Rafi Licht · Property owner
