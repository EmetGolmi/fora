Component 04

Steel-Man Banner — Neighbor's Note

Variant I · Royal Phoenician Purple

Purple is earned here — Royal Phoenician, the colour of valour and respect. Reserved exclusively for the steel-man, it signals that presenting the strongest opposing case is an honour, not a penalty.

Steel-man challenge · Neighbor's Note · Purple is platform-exclusive
⚖️
Steel-man challenge
Before posting, articulate the strongest case for the view you oppose. Not a caricature of it — the best version.
◆ Phoenician Purple — reserved for earned civic valour
Purple appears nowhere else on the platform. When a reader sees this colour, they know: this is the steel-man component. That exclusivity gives the challenge weight. The left rule mirrors the Charter aesthetic — anchored, directional, not floating.
Component 05

Tone Intercept — Community Notice

Variant I

The village elder's gentle correction. Gold cream tint with gilt left rule — warm caution, not red alarm. The community sets these norms together.

Tone intercept · Community Notice
🌡
Tone check
Your draft may read as dismissive. The community gathers in good faith — consider revising before posting.
Gilt tint and rule — warm, not alarming. The "Post anyway" option is always present and unhidden. FORA doesn't block speech; it creates a pause and asks for reflection. The community notice respects the poster's autonomy.

Component 07

Civic Scaffold Cards — Agenda Item

Variant II

Left rule creates a reading column — like a town meeting agenda handout. The colour of the rule communicates the card type before the reader sees the text.

Civic scaffold · Agenda Item layout
What it does
Shifts housing from state assignment to cooperative ownership for residents with 10+ years of continuous occupancy.
Transfiere la vivienda de asignación estatal a propiedad cooperativa.
Fiscal note
₱840M in title transfer revenue over 5 years. Implementation cost ~₱120M. Net positive by year 3.
₱840M en ingresos · costo ₱120M.
Best case
Builds property-owning citizenry. Incentivizes maintenance. State housing burden −30% within a decade.
Construye ciudadanía propietaria. Carga estatal −30%.
Worst case
Wealthier residents consolidate multiple units. Long-term low-income tenants priced out of cooperative pricing.
Los ricos consolidan. Los inquilinos pobres quedan fuera.
The left rule acts as a semantic signal before the kicker text loads. Blue = informational. Green = optimistic. Red = cautionary. Gilt = fiscal. The eye reads the colour before the label. Bilingual secondary text always in italic — never competing with the primary.
