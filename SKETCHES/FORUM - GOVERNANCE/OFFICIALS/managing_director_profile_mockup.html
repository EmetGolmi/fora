The city's chief operating officer. The Managing Director runs Philadelphia's day-to-day operations — not elected, appointed by the Mayor. If your street isn't plowed, your trash isn't picked up, or your neighborhood feels unsafe, this office is where that accountability starts. Thiel oversees roughly 20 city departments and thousands of employees.
