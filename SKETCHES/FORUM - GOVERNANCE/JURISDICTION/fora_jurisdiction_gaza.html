A Territory Under War — 70,000 Dead,
2.3 Million Displaced, No Clear End State
The Gaza war that began after Hamas's October 7 2023 attack on Israel has killed more than 70,000 Palestinians, displaced nearly the entire population, and destroyed the majority of Gaza's infrastructure. A Phase 1 ceasefire took effect October 2025. As of March 2026, Phase 1 is holding, but a permanent resolution — including the fundamental question of who governs Gaza after the war — remains unresolved.

Territory · people · history
What Gaza Is — and What It Is Not
Gaza is a 365 km² strip of land on the southeastern Mediterranean coast — one of the most densely populated territories on Earth. It is not a state. It is not formally part of Israel. Its legal status is disputed — Israel withdrew settlers and military in 2005 but maintains a blockade; international law generally considers it still occupied. Its 2.3 million residents are among the world's largest stateless populations.

The Questions Gaza Forces
Gaza is not primarily a question about Hamas. It is a question about 2.3 million people and the political future of a territory that has been under blockade, occupation, or war for most of its modern existence. These are the CivicIssues that Gaza generates.
🗳️
Who should govern Gaza after the war — and how should that authority be established?
Options range from a revitalized PA to an international mandate to a new Palestinian technocratic government to Hamas remaining in power. Every option has a constituency and every option has structural problems. This is the defining unresolved question of the conflict.

Does the scale of Gaza's destruction constitute genocide under the Genocide Convention?
The ICJ found a plausible risk of genocide in Jan 2024 but did not rule definitively. The question of legal definition, intent, and threshold is actively litigated. South Africa brought the case; Israel, US, UK dispute the characterization. FORA records all legal arguments.

Can Gaza be rebuilt under the current political framework — and who pays?
Reconstruction estimated at $40–80B. No reconstruction can proceed without a political settlement on governance. Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and Gulf states have indicated conditional willingness to fund reconstruction. The Iran-Israel war (Feb 2026) has complicated regional financing.

🌍
What is the relationship between Gaza and a Palestinian state?
Two-state solution frameworks assume a Palestinian state comprising Gaza + West Bank. Hamas and PA are rival governments. Gaza and West Bank have been governed separately since 2007. Reunification is a prerequisite for any two-state outcome — and no reunification roadmap currently exists.
