What does attendance mean?
Every senator is expected to show up and vote. 1,247 of 1,364 means Fetterman voted on 1,247 out of 1,364 total votes held while he was in office.

Missing votes isn't always avoidable — illness, family, scheduling conflicts happen. But a pattern matters.

Fetterman missed more votes during his 2023 hospitalization for depression, which he disclosed publicly.

What is party-line voting?
This measures how often a senator votes the same way as the majority of their party.

68% means Fetterman voted with most Democrats 68% of the time — and broke with them 32% of the time. The average Senate Democrat votes with their party 84% of the time.

Fetterman is one of the most independent Democrats in the Senate — he has broken with his party on immigration, Israel, and trade policy.
Senate Dem average: 84% · Fetterman: 68%
Among the most independent Democrats

Where bills live or die
Before any bill reaches a full Senate vote, it goes through a committee — a small group of senators who hold hearings, call witnesses, rewrite the bill, and decide if it moves forward.

A committee chair can kill a bill simply by never scheduling a vote. This is where the real work — and the real blocking — happens.

Fetterman's Agriculture seat means he has a direct say over food policy, nutrition programs, and farm legislation. Banking gives him influence over financial regulation.
