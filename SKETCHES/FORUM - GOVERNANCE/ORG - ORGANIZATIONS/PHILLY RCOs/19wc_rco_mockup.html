◆ About this neighborhood The 19th Ward covers parts of Fairhill and West Kensington — the center of Philadelphia's Hispanic community, anchored by the "El Centro de Oro" commercial corridor along N. 5th Street. The neighborhood is predominantly Puerto Rican, with significant Dominican, Colombian, and Mexican populations, and has one of the highest concentrations of Puerto Ricans in the continental United States. Fairhill developed through successive immigrant waves — German, then Irish, then Latino — as industries along nearby rail lines rose and fell. Today it faces dual pressures: the legacy of disinvestment from the city's deindustrialization era, and rising development pressure pushing north from Fishtown and East Kensington. Bounded roughly by Germantown Ave, Front Street, Allegheny Ave, and Cumberland Street.
◆ What is a ward committee RCO? Philadelphia's 66 political wards each elect a ward committee — Democratic and Republican committeepersons from every division (precinct). Ward committees are automatically eligible to register as RCOs, giving them an official role in zoning decisions even though their primary purpose is electoral organizing. The 19th Ward Committee serves this dual function: partisan political organizing and neighborhood zoning representation. Residents engaging on zoning issues should attend ward committee meetings and can also submit written comments directly to the PCPC.
