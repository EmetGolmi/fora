Why 10? ›
Why the threshold is 10: CCRA has a volunteer zoning committee of 5 people who review every submitted Effort. A threshold of 10 confirmed neighbors ensures the committee's time is spent on issues that have demonstrated neighborhood support — not single reports that may reflect one person's experience. Lower-threshold issues (3+) are still visible to CCRA leadership on their dashboard. This threshold applies to Efforts only — you can share, respond to, and escalate any issue at any time regardless of confirmation count.
