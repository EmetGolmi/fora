How this works: Raise an issue. Your neighbors can Concur — confirming they've seen or experienced it too. When 10 neighbors Concur, CCRA is automatically alerted to take action and the issue enters their official record. You can Share any issue at any time — including sharing to recruit the Concurrences needed to trigger the alert. We disclose all thresholds and our reasoning publicly. Why 10? ›
Why the threshold is 10: CCRA's volunteer zoning committee has capacity to formally pursue roughly 3–5 issues per month. A threshold of 10 Concurrences ensures their effort goes where the neighborhood has spoken — not where a single voice is loudest. Issues with 3+ Concurrences are visible to CCRA leadership on their internal dashboard regardless of threshold. Sharing an issue before it reaches 10 is how neighborhoods recruit the Concurrences that trigger action. The threshold is a signal of community confirmation, not a barrier to being heard.
What's happening on your block? Raise an issue for your neighborhood and CCRA to see. Be specific — a block, intersection, or address helps your neighbors find and Concur.
