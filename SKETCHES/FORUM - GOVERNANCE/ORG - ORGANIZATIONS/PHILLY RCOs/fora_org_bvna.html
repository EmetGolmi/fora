The Record — Resolved Efforts
Full history →
2023
Cianfrani Park Lighting Upgrade
Won
Coordinated with Parks & Rec to install improved lighting along the 8th & Fitzwater perimeter after multiple safety complaints. Installation completed October 2023.
2022
824–830 South 10th Street — Zoning Variance
Partial
BVNA negotiated design modifications with developer. Height reduced from 5 to 4 stories. Affordable unit commitment secured. ZBA approved with conditions.
2021
Pine & Spruce Bike Lane Ordinance — Support
Won
BVNA joined CCRA and Wash West in conditional support. City ordinance passed June 2021. Both bike lanes flipped to protected lanes.
2019
Bella Vista Slow Zone — Phase 1 Application
Referred
Application submitted Dec 2018. City awarded only 2 zones citywide in 2019 — Bella Vista not selected. Effort converted to ongoing push for pedestrian plaza and direct Traffic Engineering engagement.
2018
Fitzwater & Passyunk — Intersection Improvements
Won
Stop legend installed near Meredith School following BVNA Transportation Committee request. Loading zone near Plenty Cafe removed to improve sight lines.
