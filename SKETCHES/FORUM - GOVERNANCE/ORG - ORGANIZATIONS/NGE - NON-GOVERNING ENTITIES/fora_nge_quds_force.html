Overview
Iran's Long Arm: The Force Built to Export the Revolution
The Quds Force is not Iran's army. It is Iran's foreign policy executed by proxy — the instrument through which the Islamic Republic built Hezbollah from scratch, armed Hamas, funded the Houthis, and extended Shia political power from Beirut to Baghdad to Sana'a. For three decades it operated in the shadows. Soleimani made it famous. The 2026 war may have broken it.

Command structure · 1998–present
Three Commanders in 28 Years — Two Assassinated by the Same Adversary
The Quds Force has had three commanders since Soleimani took it from a loose intelligence unit to a regional empire. Two were killed by the US or Israel. The third has survived everything around him — which is itself the source of the espionage suspicion that may now have destroyed him from within.
AZ
Commander · 1998–2020
Qasem Soleimani
Served 22 years · Killed Jan 3, 2020 · Baghdad International Airport
Built the Quds Force from a regional intelligence unit into the primary instrument of Iranian foreign policy. Architected Hezbollah's military capabilities, the PMF in Iraq, and the "ring of fire" strategy around Israel. Considered by many analysts the second most powerful person in Iran after Khamenei. His assassination by US drone strike was the single largest blow to the Axis of Resistance before 2024.
🕯Killed by US drone strike · Jan 3, 2020

Commander · Jan 2020–present (disputed)
Esmail Qaani
Appointed by Khamenei same day Soleimani was killed · Born Mashhad, 1957
Soleimani's longtime deputy, focused on South/Central Asia and Latin America while Soleimani ran the Arab world. Known as a bureaucrat rather than a charismatic commander. Survived multiple reported assassination attempts — including, apparently, the Feb 28 strike that killed Ali Khamenei. That survival pattern triggered IRGC espionage investigation. Reportedly last seen meeting with Khamenei shortly before the fatal strike. Status as of March 15: unconfirmed — detention/execution reports unverified by Western outlets.

Axis of Resistance · Built by the Quds Force
The Proxy Network: What the Quds Force Built, and What the 2024–2026 Wars Have Taken
The Quds Force's core mission was to create a network of allied armed groups that could project Iranian power without Iranian fingerprints. For 40 years that network was remarkably durable. Since October 2023, it has been systematically dismantled — Nasrallah killed, Hamas decapitated, Houthi capabilities degraded, PMF under pressure. The Quds Force built the Axis of Resistance. Israel and the US have spent two years destroying it.

Jerusalem Force or Terror Network — The Name Reveals the Frame
The Quds Force is named for Jerusalem — al-Quds in Arabic, "the holy." That name is a declaration of purpose: liberation of Jerusalem from Israeli control is the stated mission. What Western governments call the same organization tells you exactly what frame produces what designation.
