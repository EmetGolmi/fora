★ Monday · May 11, 2026
On this day in Philadelphia
Every Philadelphian deserves to learn something new today. We do this together.
May 11 · 1880
The Free Library of Philadelphia is founded
Dr. William Pepper convinces City Councils to charter a free public library — one of the first in the United States. Pepper's argument anchored the idea that knowledge belongs to the commons. The Parkway Central building wouldn't open until 1927.
"A library is the people's university." — William Pepper, 1880
★ Born today · Philadelphia
Marian Anderson
1897 · South Philadelphia
Contralto and civil rights pioneer. In 1939, after the DAR refused her Constitution Hall, she sang from the steps of the Lincoln Memorial to 75,000 people. First Black soloist at the Metropolitan Opera.

Care for your home
The way we built Philly is a building tradition we inherited. Old homes are resilient and want maintenance — lime plaster, brick, slate, single-pane glass. Newer homes have different needs. What works on your block probably works on mine.
This is the knowledge layer, not a contractor directory. What your house is made of, what it needs, how to test it, what the code says. Find a tradesperson the old-fashioned way — your neighbor, your RCO, word of mouth. A verified-pro directory is coming, in our Commerce sector. Not yet.
