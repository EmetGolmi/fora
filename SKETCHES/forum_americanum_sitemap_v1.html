Modals & Overlays

Not standalone screens — triggered in-context
Civic Scaffold
Triggered: before engaging on any bill
What it does · Who it affects · Best/worst case · Precedent · Cohort sentiment. Must be dismissed before posting.
Source Quality Widget
Triggered: in Compose, inline on discourse
Tier S→D + ⚠flagged rating shown alongside any cited source. Legitimacy scores citations, not claims.
Tone Intercept
Triggered: before publishing a post
Forced pause. Surfaced opposing article. "Are you sure?" with a 10-second delay. Reduces reactive posting.
Steel-Man Prompt
Triggered: when composing a position
"What's the strongest argument against your position?" — optional but encouraged. Elevated display if completed.
Address Entry
Triggered: first visit, or /stack with no address
Simple address field → geocode → polygon match → government stack returned. No account required to view.
Follow Prompt
Triggered: after government stack revealed
"Follow all your officials?" One-click to populate feed. Seeding moment — highest-leverage onboarding step.
Content Flag
Triggered: flag button on any post
ContentFlag record created. Routes to moderation queue. Flagger gets confirmation; no immediate removal.
Data Alert Banner
Triggered: stale or unverified data detected
DataAlert record surfaced inline. "This record may be outdated — last verified [date]." Links to report issue.
