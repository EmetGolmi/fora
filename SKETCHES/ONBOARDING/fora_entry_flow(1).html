Step 2 · Your age

When were
you born?

FORA derives your age range from your birthdate and displays that to your community. A "45–54 · Alexandria VA" carries civic context that an anonymous post doesn't.

Why FORA asks for your birthdate
Federal law requires online platforms to verify that users are old enough to participate. We also use your birthdate to show your age range (e.g. "35–44") to your community — never your exact age or birthday. Generational voice is civic context.
Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act · 15 U.S.C. § 6501 · FTC Rule 16 C.F.R. Part 312

You entered your zip code to see your government stack. Now we bind your account to your address — so FORA knows exactly which representatives, bills, and local issues belong to you.

Why your address determines your civic stack
Every level of government — federal, state, county, city, school district — is determined by your residential address. Your congressional district, state legislative district, and local representatives are all address-specific. FORA uses the same address standards as voter registration.
Help America Vote Act · 52 U.S.C. § 20901 · National Voter Registration Act · 52 U.S.C. § 20501
