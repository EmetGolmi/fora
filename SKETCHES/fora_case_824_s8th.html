What's being proposed
Applicant proposes converting the ground-floor commercial space (currently a restaurant) to residential use — adding a third residential unit to the building. The property sits on a CMX-2 corner lot at 8th & Fitzwater. Residential use at ground level in CMX-2 requires a use variance because it removes a permitted commercial use from a commercial corridor. This is the core issue before the ZBA.

Current BVNA stance
Likely to oppose · Meeting pending
BVNA historically opposes conversion of ground-floor CMX-zoned properties on corners and commercial corridors from commercial to residential use. 824–830 S 8th is a corner CMX-2 property on Fitzwater Street, one block from the Italian Market. During construction
Street use permits

These are out of scope at the BVNA zoning meeting — but they are legally required. If a permit hasn't been pulled, report to 311.

🚛 Dumpster on street or sidewalk
Two permits may apply: a Temporary Construction Dumpster Permit from Streets Dept (call 215-686-5500) and an L&I Construction Dumpster License via eCLIPSE. Each dumpster must display an RFID medallion. No servicing between 9 PM–7 AM.
phila.gov · Construction Dumpster License ↗
🏗 Equipment on ROW (scaffolding, boom, lift, fence)
Requires a Street Closure Permit (Equipment Placement type) from the Streets Dept Right-of-Way Unit, applied online at least 10 business days in advance. If a travel lane is blocked, police presence is required and additional fees apply.
phila.gov · Street Closure Permit ↗
🏗 Crane on public right-of-way
Requires a Street Closure Permit plus a separate Crane Placement Application with a signed Police Approval Application. Tower cranes also need an L&I Tower Crane Permit (submitted via eCLIPSE with Streets permit attached). $15M liability insurance required naming City as additional insured.
phila.gov · Tower Crane Permit ↗
📬 7+ day full closures — neighbor notice required
For projects requiring a full street closure lasting 7 or more days, the contractor must notify all properties within 540 ft at least one week prior — by mail, email, or door-to-door. Notice must include project scope, duration, safety hazards, parking restrictions, detours, and contact info.
Philadelphia Code § 11-705(c.1)
📞
No permit? Report it.
Call 311 or visit phila.gov/311. Streets Dept also maintains a live permit map updated every 30 minutes — you can check whether a closure has been properly permitted before calling.
