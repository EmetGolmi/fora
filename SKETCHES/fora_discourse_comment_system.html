Amendment #1 · proposed by NoahW · Mar 15 2026
Section: stat-displaced :: scale-in-context · Status: Open for vote · 72h remaining
This is one of the fastest and most complete displacements of a civilian population in modern conflict history. 85% of a population displaced within 18 months. For comparison: the 1948 Nakba displaced ~700,000 Palestinians over a longer period. The Srebrenica genocide displaced a smaller total number.

At peak, ~85% of Gaza's population was displaced within 18 months — one of the fastest displacements in modern conflict history. The 1948 Nakba displaced ~700,000 Palestinians (a comparable percentage of the then-Palestinian population). The Jewish exodus from Arab states displaced ~850,000 Jews (1948–1972) with no UN refugee agency and no recognized right of return. The Holocaust killed 6 million Jews and displaced millions more. The Srebrenica massacre involved the killing of ~8,000 Bosniaks and smaller-scale displacement. These comparisons are offered for scale, not equivalence — each case has distinct causes, contexts, and outcomes that FORA documents separately.
Rationale: The original text compared Gaza only to the Nakba and Srebrenica, omitting major Jewish displacement events that are comparable in scale. The omission creates an asymmetric framing. The revised text adds the Mizrahi exodus and Holocaust for completeness, and adds a disclaimer that comparisons are for scale, not equivalence.
