01 · Credential Rings
What a ring means
Gold ring = third-party verified · No ring = self-reported or anonymous
A ring means one thing: a third party has confirmed this claim. Gold ring = licensing board verified, present tense. No ring = no verification — the pill still tells you what the person claims. The ring and the pill are independent of each other.

02 · Military Ribbon
Veterans only · six branches
DD-214 verified · no branch text
The ribbon bar sits beneath the circle. It carries the branch colors. Nothing else — no text, no label on the marker itself. The branch colors do all the work for anyone who recognizes them.

A veteran who is also a verified professional carries both the gold ring and the ribbon — neither displaces the other.
03 · Sponsor Center
Advertisement lives in the circle
User chose this · it is their expression
The circle is the user's content. If they accept a sponsorship, the sponsor's presence is the face itself — a branded preset they chose. The ring, the ribbon, the pill remain unchanged. The face is the only advertisement surface.

The credential ring and pill are immutable. The sponsor cannot change the ESQ. The sponsor cannot move the ribbon. They bought the face — nothing else. The "Sponsored" label appears below the pill in 7px JetBrains Mono, with the sponsor's brand dot. It is legible but not loud. The user chose this. That choice is the disclosure.
